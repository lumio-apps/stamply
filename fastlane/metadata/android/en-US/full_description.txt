Stamply tells you in seconds whether you need a visa for your next trip.

Pick your passport and your destination, and Stamply shows a clear result: visa-free, visa on arrival, electronic travel authorisation (ETA), e-Visa or visa required, plus the allowed stay where it is known.

<b>Features</b>
• Visa requirements for 199 passports and destinations
• Allowed stay in days, where available
• "Before you fly" tips for every trip
• Explore every destination for your passport, filtered by status
• Save trips and see your recent checks
• Works fully offline: all data is built into the app
• "Update data" downloads the latest visa data when you choose
• Light and dark theme

<b>Private by design</b>
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• No account or sign-in
• Your checks stay on your device
• Free and open source

<b>Please note</b>
Stamply gives general guidance only. Visa rules change often. Always confirm with the embassy or the official government website of your destination before you travel.
